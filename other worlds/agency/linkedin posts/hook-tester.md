## Hook Rules (from viral-hooks-writing-guide.md)

- Hook must land in the first 150 characters / 2-3 lines before "see more".
- Replace placeholders with specific numbers, names, industries, and details.
- Lead with specific dollar amounts, industries, or claim scenarios for insurance.
- Avoid engagement bait: "Comment YES if you agree," follow-for-follow, artificial urgency.
- One idea per post. Lead with time / money / focus, never with "AI".
- Test two hooks per week. Keep winners, retire losers.
