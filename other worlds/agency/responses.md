# LinkedIn DM Responses — Insurance Broker Discovery

## Purpose
Standardized LinkedIn DMs for Stage 4 problem discovery with insurance brokers, plus a reply log of every conversation sent. Discovery first, pitch never on the first call.

## Scripts (reuse as-is)

**Initial outreach (discovery, non-pitch):**
> Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I'm starting out in automation and researching the insurance brokerage space in Kenya. I'm not selling anything just trying to understand where brokers lose the most time. What's one manual task that takes up a lot of your time each week? Things like follow-ups, onboarding, renewals, or chasing documents. Would really appreciate your perspective. Thanks, Peter

**Follow-up (if no reply after ~4 days):**
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to skip if now's not a good time.

**Booking confirmation:**
> Thanks [Name], here's my calendar link: [Calendly link]. Looking forward to hearing where things feel manual in your brokerage.

**Post-call thank you + next step:**
> Thanks [Name] for the chat today. As discussed: [one painful task / one measured outcome]. I'll build a tiny MVP showing time saved and share it with you for feedback — no obligation.

**Objection — "not a brokerage / can't answer":**
> Understood, thanks for being straight with me [Name]. If you know anyone who runs a brokerage I'd appreciate an intro, otherwise no worries, I'll leave it here.

**Objection — "is this a sales pitch?":**
> Not at all, I'm not selling anything right now. I'm just trying to understand where brokers spend time on manual work so I can build something actually useful. Happy to hop on a 10-min call, or no pressure either way.

## Messaging style - what works

- Short, one paragraph max
- No em dashes
- Warm but direct
- Reference what they actually said (their specific pains, their situation)
- No conventional scripts - don't sound like a template
- No pitching - discovery first
- Low pressure - "no rush," "open to," "if you're open"
- Simple question at the end
- End with "Thanks, Peter"
