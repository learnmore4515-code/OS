# Brag Plan: BrokerFlow AI (Fiverr Gig Promo)

## What is this app?
BrokerFlow AI is a zero-touch document-chase dashboard for insurance brokers: a broker opens a case, the system sends the client a secure tokenised upload link on WhatsApp/SMS/email, tracks responses, and flags anything stuck over 7 days to an exception queue. Built on real n8n workflow WB-08 (document chasing escalation).

## The angle
A premium, restrained promo for a Fiverr gig selling n8n business-workflow automation. Not a generic SaaS launch — the video shows real UI from the dashboard, a real n8n workflow, and the finished gallery images, landing on a direct invitation to the buyer. Polished, earnest, B2B.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text on warm ivory: "Repetitive tasks. Fragmented tools." — then a dashboard stat card ("Awaiting docs: 6") flips into view, stating the broker's problem plainly.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Service lockup: "Business workflow automation — n8n Workflows · Integrations · Operations Systems" over the gallery cover composition.
- Proof: the real WB-08 n8n workflow — Document Chasing Trigger → Normalize → Gmail secure upload link → notify insurer/adjuster → Google Sheets tracker → Switch by escalation day (+2/+4/+7) → exception queue — nodes appearing one by one.
- Practical application: the dashboard case table with synthetic client cases, status badges, channel chips; a case moves to "Docs received" and the stat flips 0→1.

## Outro / punchline
Scope line: "Built around your existing process — WhatsApp, SMS, email, secure upload links." Then the closing invitation at full scale: "Tell me what you want to automate."

## User flow worth showing
Broker creates case → sends upload link → client uploads → broker marks docs received (stat flips 0 → 1).

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: restrained premium B2B film; the product speaks for itself
- Interpretation: 3-4 scenes stretched to ~24s across 5 beats, long holds, soft crossfades, generously letter-spaced typography, no corporate buzzwords, no invented metrics

## Duration: 24 seconds

- Role: warm professional bed with tasteful SFX
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: bed at ~0.30 volume, fade under the closing line
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset for vol-1; strong cues for the service lockup and the closing line; beat-grid for sequential node reveals; detect at composition if preset unavailable
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hero panel presence breathes with music RMS, no waveform visuals
