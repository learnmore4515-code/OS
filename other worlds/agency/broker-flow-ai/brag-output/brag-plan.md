# Brag Plan: BrokerFlow AI

## What is this app?
BrokerFlow AI is a document chase dashboard that helps Kenyan insurance brokers stop chasing clients for missing KYC, claims, and onboarding paperwork — it creates a case, sends the client a WhatsApp/SMS/email upload link, tracks responses, and flags anything stuck over 7 days as an exception.

## The angle
Insurance broking runs on paperwork that clients never send. BrokerFlow AI turns "please send your ID" from a manual chase into a zero-touch flow: broker creates a case, client uploads, broker marks it received. The joke is how mundane and painful the problem is, presented like a real SaaS dashboard.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A broker stares at a "Docs received: 0" stat while the exception queue ticks over 7 days — then a client upload lands and the number flips.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The create-case form: client name, phone, WhatsApp/SMS/Email channel, motor/commercial/fire policy class, renewal date.
- Multi-party alignment: broker + client + insurer + adjuster on one case.
- The client upload page: choose files, upload, "Documents submitted."
- Mark docs received / escalate to exception in the case modal.

## User flow worth showing
Create case → send request → client uploads documents → broker marks received (or escalates to exception >7d).

## Outro / punchline
"BrokerFlow AI. Zero-touch document intake. Stop chasing."
