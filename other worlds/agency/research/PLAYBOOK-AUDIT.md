### 9. Failure audit
What could make the playbook fail?
- **No warm contacts (0 trust map):** Path stalls at Stage 4. Mitigation: Kim should build 20 contacts before starting (former colleagues, LinkedIn, local business owners).
- **First pilot delivers no measurable result:** No testimonial → no paid client. Mitigation: pick the smallest painful task where time-saved is measurable (hours saved, errors reduced).
- **Payment/receipt barriers (Kenya):** Unable to receive Stripe/USD. Mitigation: test payment rails early; consider local-currency equivalent.
- **Guru income-claim distortion:** Kim may overestimate what's typical ($10k/mo in 90 days, $1M/30 days flagged as marketing framing). Mitigation: anchor to verified data only (Liam Ottley's $1,000 Stripe proof).
- **Scope creep / back-and-forth:** Unmaintainable with multiple clients. Mitigation: batch feedback into one iteration round (Liam Ottley 007).
- **People problem:** Perfect tech fails without client adoption. Mitigation: involve client team in discovery; frame AI as augmentation (Nick Puru 036).

No step requires money, employees, or expensive software. The path avoids Kim's stated aversion to cold outreach by deferring it until after proof.
