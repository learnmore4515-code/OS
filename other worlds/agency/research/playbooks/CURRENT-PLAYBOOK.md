## Objective
Land the FIRST paid AI-automation client (case study + testimonial + proof of delivery) within 30–60 days. Target: 1 paid client at $1,000–$2,000, then a second, then move to paid retainers.

## Do this now
1. Build trust map: Google Sheet of 20 people where trust already exists (friends/family running businesses, past co-workers, managers, clients, community contacts).
2. Run 5–10 warm discovery conversations (non-pitch): "where things feel manual or annoying in your day-to-day?"
3. Turn one conversation into a tiny FREE pilot (one measurable outcome: hours saved, errors reduced, leads captured).
4. Build tiny MVP showing time saved.
5. Propose maintenance or expansion (no hard close); get testimonial after delivered proof.

## Then do this
6. First PAID client at $1,000 case-study rate (discounted from $2,000–$3,000 typical).
7. Collect Stripe proof + testimonial + documented result.
8. Second client at $2,000–$3,000 typical using Cost Plus pricing.
9. Move to maintenance retainers ($300–$1,000/mo).

## Do not build yet
SaaS, custom code, employees, paid ads, paid outreach tooling, complex infrastructure. All deferred until after proof + revenue.

## Do not change direction unless
- Zero warm contacts after outreach → build trust map first.
- Unable to receive payment from Kenya → change payment method / local currency.
- No paid client after 20+ trust-map conversations + 2 free pilots → re-pick niche via 4-factor unfair-advantage audit.

## Success condition
1 paid client at $1,000–$2,000 + 1 testimonial + documented result + payment proof.
