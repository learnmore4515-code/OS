## Key lessons

- Niche via Hormozi criteria: purchasing power, industry growth, familiarity.
- Offer design via value equation: maximize dream outcome and perceived likelihood, minimize time delay and effort/sacrifice.
- Use guarantees and free front-end services to reduce perceived risk/effort.
- Parkinson's Law: tight deadlines force focus.
