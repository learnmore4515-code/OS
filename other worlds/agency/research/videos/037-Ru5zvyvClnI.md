## What this video is actually about
A first-person walkthrough of how the creator (Automate AI Consulting) closed a real estate agent on a $6,000 build + $1,000/month retainer with zero case studies or testimonials. The central argument: instead of lowering your price or faking proof when you have no testimonials, lead with the cost of the client's problem — quantify what their slow follow-up is already costing them per month, anchor your fee next to that number, and use an outcome-tied guarantee ("if the projected numbers aren't moving in 30 days, I keep working at no extra cost until they do"). Honesty about lacking proof, combined with pre-call research into where the business leaks leads, is what closed the deal.

## Key lessons
- When you have no testimonials, do NOT lower your price — in B2B a $500 AI system feels like a red flag, not a safe bet.
- Clients don't need proof your system works; they need to know what their problem is costing them right now, every month.
- Build your number bottom-up from the client's actual lead volume and conversion, not from a scary number you can't defend.
- Speed-to-lead matters: responding in under 5 minutes radically improves conversion; waiting an hour loses the lead to the next agent.
- Present the guarantee BEFORE they ask — it lands as standing behind the result, not covering doubt.
- When asked for case studies: be direct ("Not yet at this exact scale"), then immediately pivot to your clear process, pre-call leak mapping, and guarantee — you carry the risk, not them.
- Pre-call research (website, Google reviews, LinkedIn) finds where leads get stuck; public complaints in reviews are social proof you didn't create.

## Exact business model discussed
AI lead-gen / speed-to-lead system for a real estate agent: AI system texts every new lead back within seconds, keeps the conversation going until they're ready, and books the appointment onto the agent's calendar. Priced as a $6,000 build + $1,000/month retainer to run it.

## Customer
A "shrewd, old-school, no-BS" real estate agent (solo operator, no assistant, no follow-up tool), pulling in 80–100 leads/month from Zillow, Facebook ads, sphere, open houses — but answering in hours instead of minutes, losing closings to faster competitors.

## Problem
Slow speed-to-lead: leads sit for hours or are never contacted; in real estate a lead has a shelf life of minutes. The agent was losing at least one closing per month (~$7,000/month after broker split) due to slow follow-up, plus wasting ad dollars on leads left to rot.

## Offer
"We text every new lead back within seconds, day or night, keep the conversation going with AI until they're ready, and book the appointment straight onto your calendar. The build is $6,000 and it's $1,000 a month to run it." Guarantee: if projected numbers aren't moving in the first 30 days, keep working at no extra cost until they do — outcome defined up front (seconds-to-response, appointments on calendar without agent involvement).

## Exact tactics
- Run the monthly-loss number for the prospect's exact situation before the call.
- Read their Google reviews for public complaints about slow replies; bring a screenshot to the call.
- Open the call naming their leak, not your credentials.
- Answer the case-study question honestly, then pivot to process + guarantee.
- Guarantee tied to a clear, pre-defined result (seconds-to-response, appointments on calendar).

## Mistakes / warnings
- Lowering price to $500 when you have no proof is a mistake (red flag).
- Full-refund guarantees backfire (they make the prospect picture failure) — use an outcome-tied, work-until-results guarantee instead.
- Faking proof or stretching an unrelated project into a case study will be caught by a sharp buyer and destroy trust.
