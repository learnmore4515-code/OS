## What this video is actually about
A general educational overview of the AI consulting business model and its economics. An AI consultant helps businesses understand where AI creates value, which tools fit, how to implement, and how employees use it — selling a solution to a business problem, not an AI tool. Emphasizes picking a niche (industry or problem-based) so the offer is specific ("I help real estate companies automate lead follow-up and customer communication using AI"). Services: AI audit, implementation, training (combinable into a structured solution). Pricing: hourly, per-project, or monthly retainer — no universal answer; depends on experience, niche, complexity, value delivered, client. Credibility built via educational content, case studies, certifications, and demonstration projects (fictional business → real case studies). First client via warm network (former colleagues, friends who own companies, local businesses) with problem-specific messaging; LinkedIn for B2B ("teach before you sell"; claims 70% of IT screening and 70% of IT hiring happen via LinkedIn/AI tools). Legal foundation: contracts, separate business bank account, invoices, payment terms, data handling (don't upload confidential info, human review, AI makes mistakes). Economics: low startup costs but not zero (software, registration, accounting, insurance, marketing); biggest investment is time. Productize over time: repeatable offer → systems → document → hire/outsource → manage rather than do everything. Start with one customer type, one painful problem, one clear offer, one demonstration, one client; confirm people will pay before building a big company.

## Key lessons
- Sell business solutions/outcomes, not AI tools.
- Niche down (industry or problem) to make the offer specific and credible.
- Services: audit → implementation → training, combinable.
- Pricing models: hourly / per-project / retainer; no universal rate.
- Credibility via content, demos, case studies — not degrees.
- First clients from warm network with problem-led messaging.
- Legal/data-handling basics before taking paying clients.
- Productize to scale; validate with one paying client before scaling.
