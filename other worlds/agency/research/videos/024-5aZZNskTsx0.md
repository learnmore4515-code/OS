## Exact tactics
- 80/20 split in interviews (listen more, talk less); constantly ask "why" and "how"; record calls with permission.
- Ask magic-wand questions ("if you could fix one workflow issue," "automate one thing") to surface inefficiencies.
- Interview both leadership (goals/impact) and end users (day-to-day reality); the gap between them is where money is made.
- Process map into acquisition/delivery/support; tag time syncs and quality risks.
- Opportunity matrix: plot each item by business impact vs implementation effort; prioritize quick wins (low effort, high impact).
- Present findings via five-slide deck with ROI calculator; co-create prioritization; end with next steps.
- Cold outbound email framework: name, personalization, offer, social proof, call to action; never send without first name.
- Mass personalization at scale via Clay + AI + instantly.ai/Smartly.ai/heyreach.
- Dream 100: list 100 ideal clients/partners with job title, company size, industry, vertical, geography, exclusions.
- Build client agents as manager + sub-agent teams (not one monolithic agent) so they don't break.
- Speaker claims his systems take ~15 minutes/week to run once built.

## Mistakes / warnings
- Don't sell a product first (e.g., "we build chatbots, now find someone to buy"); audit first, then prescribe.
- Don't do edge-cased or "bleeding edge" custom AI builds; overengineered solutions with marginal payoff are not worth it.
- Don't automate tasks humans still do better/faster than AI.
- Maintenance post-implementation is mandatory, not optional; 95% of AI projects fail partly because of no maintenance.
- Be conservative in ROI estimations; address objections; present math collaboratively.
- Revenue-uplift assumptions may not materialize (freed-up support staff won't necessarily generate sales; headcount cuts may be the real lever).
- AI landscape changes daily; what works today may be outdated tomorrow; execution and staying ahead matter more than knowing the process.
- Do not ask about visions/goals in discovery interviews ("wrong move"); hunt for broken stuff/inefficiencies.
