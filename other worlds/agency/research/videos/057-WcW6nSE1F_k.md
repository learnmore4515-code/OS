## What this video is actually about
Zubair Trabzada's reality check on getting a first AI agency client. Dismisses "$200k–$300k/month" teen-creator claims as nonsense. Core problem isn't skills — it's focus: people pitch AI to gyms, dentists, e-commerce, real estate in sequence and get nowhere. Path: pick ONE niche (even an unsexy one), spend ~1 week learning its pain points (forums, reviews, YouTube comments — Reddit is a gold mine), build ONE small AI solution (doesn't need to be perfect), reach out to 5–10 people, offer it free or cheap just to prove it works; don't worry about pricing/scaling/logos yet — the only job is getting ONE person to say yes. Runs a private paid community "AI Workshop" with a step-by-step daily accountability guide to the first client (not flashy, no $100k in 30 days promise). Evidence: firsthand ("over a year" agency + community) but speaker-stated and unverified; includes a community pitch.

## Key lessons
- Focus, not skill, is the blocker to the first client.
- One niche, one week of research, one small imperfect solution, 5–10 free/cheap offers → first yes.
- Price/scale/logo don't matter before the first yes.
- Reddit/forums/reviews are good sources for niche pain points.
- Community pitch: AI Workshop (paid, step-by-step accountability).
