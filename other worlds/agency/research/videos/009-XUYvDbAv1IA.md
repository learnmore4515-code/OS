## What this video is actually about
Zubair Trabzada debunks YouTube income claims by 18–19 year old creators who say they make over $200,000–$300,000 a month from AI automation agencies selling to small businesses. He says these creators are deliberately lying to people, shares his own real numbers from running an AI agency for a year, and warns that the hype hurts genuine people trying to enter the AI automation field. It is a caution/reality-check video, not a how-to.

## Key lessons
- Claims of $200K–$300K/month from AI automations by teen creators are lies; the speaker says "They're lying to you 100%" and that he is talking from experience.
- Small businesses do not pay that much for AI automations ("I guarantee you they are not paying you that much money").
- Making real money at that level requires actual companies with sales teams and long sales cycles — not solo teenagers.
- Making $200K–$300K/month is possible, but the chances are "probably .1%".
- It is not a lottery or joke; making money requires working extremely hard for a long time.
- If someone were actually making over $300K in automation, they would not have time to make YouTube videos and sell newsletters — so the guru model "doesn't make any sense."
- The speaker's own experience: AI agency for the past year; over $100,000 total made, but the majority came from his Skool community (teaching n8n and AI automations), not from the AI agency.
- His best month from selling AI automations: $30,000 (the highest). Current average: $10,000–$15,000/month from his consultancy, because most of his focus is on his school community.
- His YouTube traction came from being one of the first creators to make tutorials on n8n, which he calls the most popular AI automation tool in the market.
