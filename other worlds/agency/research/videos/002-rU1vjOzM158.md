He then gives the closed client(s) away to a viewer via a comment/like/subscribe/Instagram-story-tag entry challenge. He states he has "personally made hundreds of thousands of dollars online with the AI agency business model as a teenager" (speaker claim, unverified) and previously sold to realtors ("That's the agency I had for a while").

## Exact business model discussed
AI automation/done-for-you agency serving real estate agents: run Facebook/Instagram (Meta) ads to a booking-page funnel, use an AI caller to qualify leads and book them into the agent's calendar, deliver 10 listing appointments in 90 days. Three-month terms. Pricing $5,000 upfront or $2,000/month split ($6,000 over 3 months), both with a money-back guarantee if 10 listing appointments aren't delivered. Client pays their own ad spend. Agency builds and hands off the system (GHL snapshot, funnels, automations, AI agent) which the client keeps for life. Positioned as "growth partner" where the agency does lead gen/appointment setting and the agent just closes deals.

## Customer
Real estate agents and realtors in the United States (not limited to one city — broader TAM). Age 25+, primarily 30–45, secondary 45–55. Established agents (he doesn't want to work with newbies; looked for 7+ years experience), who have money, are tired of cold calling and flyers, rely on referrals that have slowed, don't have time to run ads, find Zillow expensive, and want consistent qualified appointments with motivated buyers/sellers. Examples on calls: "Kent" and "Trey" (Sacramento, 4 years licensed, 10 deals in 2024, 9 in 2023, avg home price $480–485K, 3% commission, ~$130–150K GCI, referrals only, goal of 20 deals) and "Kevin" (Ohio, 5 years, 14 transactions last year, 75/25 paid split).

## Problem
Realtors hate cold calling and flyers; they'd rather have lead gen automated. Referrals have slowed down. They don't have time to run ads. Zillow leads are expensive. Their goal is listing appointments and buyer appointments — more qualified appointments with motivated buyers and sellers, consistently, without cold calling or chasing leads. They need follow-up on leads (leads ghost; speed-to-lead matters).

## Offer
"We will get you 10 listing appointments in the next 90 days using our AI system or you do not pay." He clarifies this isn't a refund necessarily — it means they work for free until they deliver the results. One-sentence offer explanation framing. Components: Facebook/Instagram ads to a booking page; AI caller calling leads (10 min, 6 hrs, 12 hrs, 24 hrs after opt-in) to qualify and book into calendar; pre-call video; one-on-one check-in calls every other week; ad creatives and copy; 24/7 support/monitoring; KPI projections. Scarcity: limited to one agent per city. Pricing variants: $5,000 upfront (90 days) or split pay $2,000/month (3 months); client funds own ad spend ($20–25/day minimum recommended, he tested $30/day). He discounted upfront to $4,000 on a live call to close.

## Acquisition
Paid Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram). Three video ads made with Arc Ads (3 credits = ~$30) plus three static image ads made in Canva (1080×1080), split-tested. Started with campaign budget optimization, then switched to ad set budget ($50/day × 3 ad sets = $150/day), paused underperforming sets, went static-only after video ads consumed budget at high cost per appointment ($93 vs ~$20–30 for static). Funnel: ad → booking-page landing page (straight to calendar, no separate opt-in page), pre-call video on thank-you page. Pixel and lead event set up. He explicitly rejects pure cold outreach for this challenge (recommends ads if you can spend $20–25/day bare minimum; if 15 with no money, do outreach instead). Entry challenge for viewers: subscribe, like, comment, tag him on Instagram story (@Carson Reed45) to win one of the closed clients.

## Sales
One-call close framework (screening/intro call → demo). Discovery questions: how many deals/year, GCI target, average home price, commission split, current lead sources, past marketing spend. Three-pillar pitch: (1) social media ads (static image ads, low cost per lead), (2) AI caller that qualifies and books leads 24/7, (3) 24/7 support + every-other-week check-in calls. Guarantee removes risk ("work for free until we deliver"). ROI framing: one listing deal ≈ $14,000 commission at $550K avg home price × 2.5%, so converting one of 10 appointments ≈ 3x the $5,000 fee. Objection handling on price: offered split pay and discounted upfront to $4,000 on the call. Closed: Trey (day 5, split pay $2,000/month); Kent (day 7 follow-up, paid $2,500 that day after initially fumbling the call on day 5). Kevin (Ohio) — not interested, questioned affordability. No-shows and reschedules occurred; follow-ups "usually never never close" per him (Kent was an exception).

## Fulfillment
~7-day build period: onboarding call, get access to client's Facebook/Instagram and calendar, build ad creatives, build the AI caller, connect domain, set up automations/funnels/pipeline in GHL via imported snapshot, launch ads, then slot appointments into the agent's calendar. Agent takes the appointments in person. Agency handles everything else; client keeps the AI system after the term. Onboarding call scheduled (e.g., Monday 10 a.m. for Trey). He notes he normally has a team/closers do fulfillment and sales; he did it himself for the challenge.

- That 2 clients / $4,500 collected in 6 days is typical or guaranteed (speaker's prior experience, aged ad account, 8–12 hrs/day).
- That the "$11,000 total revenue" figure is accurate (speaker-stated, inconsistent math).
- That cold outreach doesn't work — he simply chose paid ads for this challenge.
- That any beginner can close at $5,000 upfront without sales skill — he fumbled the first call and normally uses closers.
- That the guarantee is a cash refund — he frames it as "work for free until results," probably not a refund.
