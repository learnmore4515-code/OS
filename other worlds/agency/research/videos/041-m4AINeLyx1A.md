## What this video is actually about
Day 17 of Mani's challenge: a technical tutorial on setting up cold email outreach for ~$1. Buy a disposable domain on Namecheap (~$1/year; do NOT use your main domain — spam risk), get 3 mailboxes with a 2-month free Private Email trial (~$0 with the trial; ~$30–50/year for 3 mailboxes otherwise). Configure MX/SPF/DKIM DNS records (MX priority 10, etc.). Connect to Instantly.ai (no credit card required; 2 email accounts on the free plan) via SMTP/IMAP (imap.privateemail.com / smtp.privateemail.com) and warm up the domain. Build a campaign with Instantly's lead finder: job titles (CEO, director of marketing, head of marketing), US, real estate/commercial real estate, 0–25 employees, $1M–$10M revenue → 25 free leads; add to campaign. Campaign structure: 3 emails with a 5-day wait between step 1 and the follow-up. Says scripts/resources/templates are linked below and will be uploaded in coming days; chatbot templates available now. No client results or revenue stated.

## Key lessons
- Use a disposable domain for cold email to protect your main domain from spam flagging.
- Cold email infrastructure can be set up for under ~$50/year (domain + mailboxes).
- Instantly.ai free plan connects 2 accounts; no credit card to start.
- Niche the lead list (titles, location, industry, employees, revenue) before pulling leads.
- Multi-step email campaign (initial + follow-ups spaced days apart).
