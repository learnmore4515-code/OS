## What this video is actually about
Liam Ottley walks through, step by step, how he serviced his first AI automation agency client as a beginner, using a real client from his own business (Morningside AI / Morningside Ventures, Dubai) that he signed and delivered for. Covers the full client servicing lifecycle: planning, building, reviewing, deploying, and handing over, plus a bonus module on 5x revenue per customer via upsells, business audits, workshops, and monthly retainers. Recorded from Aruba (Caribbean). All of Liam's leads at the time of recording were coming from YouTube.

## Key lessons
- Start with chatbots as the low-hanging fruit for a first client; don't bite off more than you can chew when you're green/new to delivery.
- Your job is to come in as a consultant: analyze how to reduce expenses and increase ROI or increase revenue by applying AI tools to the business.
- A single outward-facing chatbot can cover multiple value bases: lead nurturing, lead capture, conversion-event push, and customer support via a custom knowledge base.
- Gather requirements before building (objectives, conversation flow, information to capture, conversion events), design the flow in Figma, and validate it with the client before building.
- Batch client feedback (one batch of iterations) rather than constant back-and-forth, which becomes unmaintainable with multiple clients.
- Transfer the bot to the client's own platform account so they are billed and responsible, not you.
- Proof builds trust: Liam showed his Stripe dashboard payment proof on screen.
- Use the chatbot as a Trojan horse to earn trust, then upsell more automations, audits, workshops, and retainers.

## Offer
Done-for-you outward-facing AI chatbot covering four value areas:
1. Lead nurturing — helps site visitors find the right products/services matched to them personally
2. Lead capture — conversation collects the visitor's intentions and contact info (email/phone number)
3. Conversion event — pushes to an external landing page or a link to book a call/trial
4. Customer support — custom knowledge base answering questions from the client's Q&A content
Tech stack: Botpress (chatbot logic, messaging, conversation flow) + Stack AI via API (knowledge base document search, GPT 3.5 Turbo) for better KB querying than Botpress offers; GPT-4 / better LLM as fallback. Custom code blocks/JavaScript used in Botpress to condense logic. V2 planned: lead-capture enrichment (email + phone) for email/SMS marketing of enriched leads with clear intent.

## Fulfillment
Phases: planning → building → reviewing → deploying → handing over (signing is the prerequisite, "phase 0").
1. Planning: identify client needs and chatbot value; request requirements (custom knowledge base supplied as Q&A pairs); map the conversation flow in Figma (questions asked, information captured, open loops querying the KB); get client validation before building.
2. Building: Botpress as the base (nodes, transitions, messages, information capture saved to variables, custom code blocks / a big JavaScript block to condense logic); Stack AI via API for knowledge base queries (document search, GPT 3.5 Turbo); fallback chain KB → Stack AI → "I don't have an answer for that, can you please try again" (infinite loop so the customer can ask unlimited questions); after every answer, re-push the conversion event (book a trial / go to landing page).
3. Reviewing: send a Botpress testing URL; client stress tests and returns one batched feedback document with screenshots rather than constant back-and-forth.
4. Deploying: client creates a Botpress account and adds the builder as a collaborator; the builder transfers the bot to the client's account (option appears in the dashboard); client is then billed/responsible; builder can still do small tweaks. Embed via Integrations → Web Chat → Pre configured → script URL added to the client's website; CSS/styling controllable (advanced, for another day).
5. Handing over: once transferred, the bot lives on the client's account; it "doesn't count as an agency until you've hired people," but it's freelance delivery experience.

## Mistakes / warnings
- Don't bite off more than you can chew when you're new/green delivering services — stick to chatbots for the first client.
- Don't rely solely on YouTube / personal brand for leads; build an outbound method that works independently (Liam was developing one).
- Avoid constant back-and-forth with clients during review; use batched iterations — otherwise it becomes a nightmare with many clients.
- Botpress's native knowledge base querying "is not that great," which is why Stack AI was added via API.
