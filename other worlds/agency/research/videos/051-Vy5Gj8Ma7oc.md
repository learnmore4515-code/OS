## What this video is actually about
Day 6: a small win — a testimonial for an e-commerce chatbot built 2 weeks ago, for free, for the CEO of his previous company (a supplement e-commerce store). Built with Voiceflow + Zapier. Customer-service-focused KB (~4MB, so Voiceflow fit; no Flowise/Stack AI needed), lead capturing via Zapier into an email sequence, and product-based coupons triggered by questions. Also built a giveaway campaign via chatbot (QR code → giveaway page; bot verifies order number, randomizes a reward, suggests products). Not live yet (launching in 2–3 weeks); early testing smooth. Client-stated KPI: saving 60–80% on customer service effort and time (early, unverified). Strategy: build the first 3–5 bots free for people he already knows (warm network) to lock down the KPI/case study, then expand via "carpet bomb and hitman targeting" and referrals (friends of friends via LinkedIn/Facebook). Evidence: self-stated, unverified; warm-network-first case.
