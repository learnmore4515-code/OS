## What this video is actually about
Liam Ottley explains why the AI automation agency (AAA) model is the best AI business to start if you're broke. The core thesis: the best way for an aspiring entrepreneur with little to no skills or money to make money with AI is to facilitate AI adoption and help businesses apply the technology. He positions the AAA as a zero-startup-cost, beginner-friendly on-ramp that requires no coding, using low-code/no-code tools to deliver chatbots and automations to small businesses.

## Key lessons
- The historical pattern: when a new technology takes off (World Wide Web, smartphones, social media), smart entrepreneurs build agencies to help businesses adopt it — web dev companies, app dev companies, social media marketing agencies. When AI took off, smart entrepreneurs build AI automation agencies.
- The AAA model is presented as the biggest, most beginner-friendly opportunity to transition from AI spectator to active participant.
- Financial and location freedom takes time, effort, and sacrifice; there is no way around it. The lever you can pull is selecting the right business model at the right time — when competition is low and emotions are high.
- Competition for selling AI automation services is "basically zero"; the early mover advantage shrinks by the day. AAA in 2023 is claimed to be equivalent to SMMA in 2017 (completely untapped).
- You do not need to code: service delivery can be done with no-code/low-code tools (Zapier for automations; Botpress and Stack AI for AI chatbots).
- The offer is "completely fresh" — businesses have never been offered AI automation services before, and business owners generally don't know what AI can do for them or how to integrate it.
- Someone with zero sales experience can call or DM a business and close the sale because the offer is so untapped.

## Exact business model discussed
AI Automation Agency (AAA): a business that focuses on integrating AI technology into businesses, with services typically aimed at automating processes, boosting productivity, and increasing profits. Service delivery is done with no-code or low-code tools, meaning anyone can start an AAA even without a development background. The specific delivery vehicle emphasized is customized AI chatbots built on Botpress and Stack AI, plus automations via Zapier. The model originated from Liam's own company Morningside AI creating a lower-tier, affordable offer for small businesses that couldn't afford custom-coded development.

## Customer
Small businesses that need basic, affordable AI applications (chatbots, automations) but cannot afford custom-coded development. Evidence: Liam's company Morningside AI was strictly an AI development company building custom coded applications, and they were eventually ignoring any leads under $5,000 because small businesses coming in needing basic AI applications "didn't have the money for the solution." The broader target is business owners in general who have never been offered AI automation services and don't know how AI could help their business.

## Problem
1) Small businesses need basic AI applications but custom-coded development is too expensive (Morningside ignored leads under $5,000; ignored 20+ leads in one week because it had no offer for them).
2) Business owners have no clue what AI can do for their business or how to integrate it.
3) AI automation is a completely fresh offer these businesses have never been offered before (unlike saturated SMMA marketing services, which have become a race to the bottom on price).

## Offer
A lower-tier AI automation offer built on low-code/no-code bot builders — primarily customized chatbots for small businesses. The offer is described as a fresh, untapped proposition that business owners respond enthusiastically to ("wow that sounds amazing," "well yeah I'd love to try that").
