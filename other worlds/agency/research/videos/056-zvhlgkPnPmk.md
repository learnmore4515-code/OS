## What this video is actually about
A niche-specific (SAP/ERP) warning that AI + clean core + self-service are eliminating the SAP functional consultant role. Speaker: 25 years leading SAP implementations (~$700M across 100 orgs, government/defense/Fortune 500); clients suddenly stopped hiring functional consultants (one support-contract renewal: 14 → 4 consultants, same scope). Three shifts: (1) AI automating routine work — SAP Joule (2,100 embedded AI skills; 15 specialized AI agents at SAP Connect 2025; Joule Studio GA late 2025); if AI covers 60% of config and a junior handles 40%, the client needs 1 senior + AI + 1 junior instead of 5 seniors — "a math problem"; (2) clean core killing customizations (S/4HANA 2025 clean-core rating A–D; ABAP Cloud syntax enforcement; RISE methodology dashboard) — custom work is now technical debt; business fits SAP, not vice versa; (3) system learning to talk to business directly (Fiori, embedded analytics, Joule natural language, self-service workflows) — the translator role shrinks. Timelines: SD/MM config ~12 months; FI/CO ~18 months; PP/PM/QM ~24 months; regulated-industry specialists 3–4 years. Five common excuses don't hold up; certifications aren't a hiring criterion; learning ABAP is a sideways move into a pressured role; waiting is the worst choice. Pivot: stop being a "functional consultant" (implements decisions); become a transformation advisor / business architect / process strategist who shapes decisions — speak both business and SAP.

## Key lessons
- AI + clean core + self-service together eliminate the routine SAP functional-consultant role.
- The role is being hollowed from the bottom up (junior config work automated first).
- Custom SAP configuration is now technical debt under clean core.
- Regulated/industry specialists have a longer runway (3–4 years) but not immunity.
- Pivot from "implementer of decisions" to "shaper of decisions" (transformation advisor/business architect).
- Certifications don't drive hiring decisions; problem-solving in front of executives does.
