## Fulfillment
All fulfillment is via no-code tools; speaker states he delivers these himself and gives step-by-step instructions:
1. Cold outreach: lead sheet with LinkedIn URLs → Zapier pulls a row → Phantom Buster scrapes the LinkedIn profile → data injected into a prompted ChatGPT window ("expert cold outreach message writer") → output message → connect to email delivery platform → send email to lead.
2. Staff training: request training docs/FAQs from owner → ingest into custom knowledge base in Botpress or Stack AI → deploy chatbot to WhatsApp, Telegram, or web URL.
3. Plan generator: collect past plans from client → load into Stack AI vector database → prompt to generate plans from retrieved examples → deploy chatbot on Stack AI or Botpress → client enters criteria (e.g., vegan meal plan for a 40-year-old woman losing 10 pounds) → chatbot retrieves similar plans, injects into prompt, generates output.
4. Onboarding: workshop call to map the current onboarding system (contracts, invoicing, communication channels, background steps) → condense into a plan → map in Figma or directly in Botpress → connect info capture, invoicing, contract sending/generation, Slack channels, Trello boards → deploy to the client's website.
5. Airbnb: get support/FAQ documents for a property → ingest into custom knowledge base in Botpress or Stack AI → deploy bot to WhatsApp or Telegram → host gives the Telegram handle/WhatsApp number to guests for support queries.
