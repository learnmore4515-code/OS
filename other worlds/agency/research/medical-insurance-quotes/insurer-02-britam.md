## Product structure
- **Five plan tiers:** Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Essential.
- Tiered structure: benefits, hospital access, and condition sub-limits vary by tier.
- "Cover for life — join up to age 80, stay protected for life."

## Benefits covered (by tier)
- **Inpatient limits:** KSh 300K → KSh 10M across all five plans (varies by tier).
- **Outpatient care:** "Seamless — no approvals needed" across all five plans.
- **Hospital access:** Platinum/Gold/Silver = open panel (all hospitals incl. top private e.g. Aga Khan, Nairobi, MP Shah, Mater). Bronze = Tier 2 & 3 medium-cost hospitals. Essential = mission & government hospitals and low-cost facilities.
- **Chronic conditions:** Platinum = newly diagnosed sub-limit first 2 years, then full cover year 3; pre-existing/cancer/HIV/AIDS/congenital full cover after waiting period. Gold = higher sub-limits than Silver; pre-existing/cancer/HIV/AIDS/congenital subject to sub-limits. Silver/Bronze/Essential = newly diagnosed and pre-existing conditions (incl. cancer, HIV/AIDS) subject to sub-limits.
- **Critical Illness Cash Benefit:** Platinum & Gold cover all inpatient limits; Silver covers 5M & 10M limits only; Bronze & Essential not included.
- **Mental health & psychiatric:** Platinum & Gold comprehensive (incl. therapy, inpatient psychiatric care, alcohol & substance rehab). Silver/Bronze/Essential = therapy + inpatient psychiatric care, exclude alcohol/substance rehab.
- **Annual wellness checks:** Included for all tiers as part of inpatient cover.
