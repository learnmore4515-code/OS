## Product structure
- Marketed as “Premium Protection for Your Whole Family, From Everyday Visits to Emergencies.”
- Comprehensive individual and family health cover.
- **Six flexible plan options**, built to grow with family needs; access to quality healthcare across East Africa and beyond.

## Benefits covered
- Inpatient cover up to **KSh 10 million** (overall cap stated).
- Optional outpatient, maternity, dental, and optical benefits.
- Cancer, HIV, chronic, congenital, and psychiatric conditions covered.
- Annual checkups and vaccinations included.
- Telemedicine, drug delivery, wellness coaching.
- Overseas treatment on credit basis plus regional coverage.
- Shared outpatient options under Royal and Prestige plans.
- Wellness services: pharmacy-based teleconsultations, doorstep drug delivery, home-based care, mental wellness support, 24/7 care navigation, community health programs.

- Some benefits subject to waiting period; refer to policy document.

- Pre-existing conditions may be covered after a waiting period, typically **12 months**.
