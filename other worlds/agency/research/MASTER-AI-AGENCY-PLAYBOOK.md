# PART 1 — THE PATH

**The single recommended progression (service-first AI automation agency):**

1. **Find a valuable problem** — businesses need help applying AI (chatbots, automations, audits) to increase productivity/revenue but don't know how to plan, build, deploy, hand over (Liam Ottley 007).
2. **Choose a customer** — 4-factor unfair-advantage audit: business access > experience/connections > interest. Interest alone is weakest (Liam Ottley 015).
3. **Create an offer** — one repeatable done-for-you offer (outward-facing chatbot / audit / automation), fixed/package pricing, clear and easy to buy (Liam Ottley 007, 014; Eliot Prince 025).
4. **Get the first customer** — warm trust map (20 people) → discovery conversations → free pilot → paid maintenance/expansion (Nate Herk 061). Discount to case-study rate for proof (Liam Ottley 007).
5. **Deliver the result** — no-code/low-code (Botpress + Stack AI), phases planning→build→review→deploy→handover (Liam Ottley 007).
6. **Get proof** — Stripe dashboard proof, testimonials, documented results (hours saved, errors reduced) (Liam Ottley 007).
7. **Improve the offer** — chatbot as Trojan horse → upsell automations, audits, workshops, retainers (Liam Ottley 007). Quick wins first (Liam Ottley 033).
8. **Get more customers** — warm network first; cold outreach with volume only AFTER proof (Nate Herk 061; Carson Reed 002; Andrew Dunn 024).
9. **Productize delivery** — quick-win infrastructure, five-layer AI core, SOPs, free frameworks as lead magnets (Nick Puru 036; Liam Ottley 020).
10. **Build systems** — diagnosis-first consulting, discovery-first sales, 10x ROI pricing (Eliot Prince 025; Nate Herk 062).
11. **Hire/automate** — delegate to developers or white-label implementers once volume exists (Eliot Prince 025; Nick Saraev 026).
12. **Service → Software** — consulting → agency → SaaS only after repeatable service, capital, and technical capability (Liam Ottley 019; Nick Saraev 026).

**Primary path: service-first AI automation agency.** No processed source recommends starting with SaaS from $0.

# PART 2 — WHY THIS PATH (evidence)

- **Service-first is unanimous** across 10+ processed creators (Liam Ottley ×10+, Andrew Dunn 024, Eliot Prince 025, Nick Saraev 026, Nick Puru 036, Nicole McCain 063). No source recommends SaaS from $0. → evidence-matrix.md.
- **Warm-network first client** is the lowest-risk $0 start (Nate Herk 004, 061; Liam Ottley 015). Cold outreach requires volume + 4+ months runway and has a high quit rate (Albert Olgaard 028 took 4 months).
- **Concrete, verified numbers exist:** Liam Ottley's $1,000 case-study chatbot with Stripe dashboard proof (3,000 Dirhams = $1,000) is the strongest evidence in the corpus (007). $2,000–$3,000 typical project; $300–$1,000/mo maintenance; $3,000–$8,000/mo retainer target (unverified forward claim).
- **Pricing ladder is consistent:** free/at-cost first projects → Cost Plus ($30–50/hr × hours × markup, start 2x → 3x–7x; 50% upfront/50% completion) → value-based/10x ROI after proof (Liam Ottley 017; Nate Herk 008; Nick Puru 036).
- **No-code/low-code delivery** (Botpress, Stack AI, Voiceflow, make.com, n8n) means $0 delivery cost on first projects (Liam Ottley 007, 020, 021).
- **Hormozi offer/sales principles** (064, supporting layer) connect directly: value equation, risk reversal, guarantee, offer layers (scarcity/urgency/bonuses/guarantees — Liam Ottley 020).

**Questionable claims flagged (do not build on these):** $200K–$300K/month guru claims (Zubair Trabzada 009 — odds ~0.1%); $10k/month in 90 days (marketing framing); $1M AIAA in 30 days (Mani Kanasani series, mid-challenge, not outcome); $3,000–$8,000/mo retainer (single source, unverified).

# PART 3 — THE BUSINESS MODEL

**Done-for-you AI Automation Agency (AIAA) / freelance AI-implementation service.**

- **Product:** one repeatable done-for-you offer solving one painful problem. Entry offer: outward-facing AI chatbot covering lead nurturing, lead capture, conversion event, customer support (Liam Ottley 007). Alternative: AI audit/assessment (Eliot Prince 025), done-for-you automation (Nick Puru 036).
- **Customer:** small-to-mid businesses (5–500 employees, ~$500K–$10M revenue) that want AI but have no time and are ignored by big consulting firms (Liam Ottley 015, 020, 033).
- **Revenue:** fixed project upfront ($1,000 case-study → $2,000–$3,000 typical), maintenance retainers ($300–$1,000/mo), infrastructure upsells ($3,000–$8,000/mo target once 10–20 automations).
- **Cost structure:** near-zero delivery cost on first projects (no-code free tiers). Main costs: time, internet, optional paid outreach tooling later.
- **Stages:** consulting → agency → SaaS only after repeatable service (Liam Ottley 019).

# PART 4 — CUSTOMER

**Who we serve:** small businesses that can benefit from an outward-facing chatbot/automation but don't know how to plan/build/deploy/hand over an AI system. The founder acts as consultant: reduce expenses, increase ROI/revenue (Liam Ottley 007).

**Niche selection (4-factor unfair-advantage audit, STRICT priority):**
1. **Business access** — your own or a friend/family business, low-pressure sandbox, expect ~7 pivots to PMF (citing Startup Genome).
2. **Experience + connections** — know the industry's painful problems; can sell into it.
3. **Experience or connections alone.**
4. **Interest alone** — weakest; "nice to have / jet fuel" only if nothing else.

**Do NOT pick a niche "because there's money in it."** Pick the one you can actually reach and whose painful problem you can articulate. Target market: mid-market (50–500 employees) for done-for-you transformation (Liam Ottley 033).

# PART 5 — PROBLEM

**Problem:** Businesses need AI applied (chatbots, automations, audits) to increase productivity and revenue but don't know how to plan, build, deploy, or hand over an AI system. Founder's job: consultative — figure out how to reduce expenses and increase ROI/revenue by applying AI tools (Liam Ottley 007).

**Real problem is upstream of the named symptom:** property-management client wanted tenant-communication automation; discovery found 30% of inbound leads lost to 3–4 hour response times — automating the symptom ($30K system) while losing $200K/year in missed leases. "You can't automate what you don't understand" (Nick Puru 036).

# PART 6 — OFFER

**Exactly what we sell:** one repeatable done-for-you offer.

**Entry offer (outward-facing AI chatbot) — four value areas (Liam Ottley 007):**
1. Lead nurturing — match site visitors to products/services personally
2. Lead capture — conversation collects intentions + email/phone
3. Conversion event — push to external landing page / booking link
4. Customer support — custom knowledge base from client Q&A

**Tech stack:** Botpress (logic/flow) + Stack AI via API (KB document search, GPT-3.5 Turbo) + Figma (flow design) + custom code blocks in Botpress (Liam Ottley 007).

**Offer layers (Hormozi 064 via Liam Ottley 020):** scarcity ("only 3 clients for July"), urgency ("first month free if bought today"), guarantee ("don't pay unless happy"), bonus. "Selling them $100 for $20."

**Alternative offers:** AI audit/assessment → implementation → maintenance (Eliot Prince 025 audits $5K–15K); done-for-you automation (Nick Puru 036 $81K case study).

# PART 7 — ACQUISITION

**Exactly how we get customers: warm network / referral-led FIRST.**

1. **Trust map:** Google Sheet of 20 people where trust already exists (friends/family running businesses, past co-workers, managers, clients, community/Slack/Discord contacts, friends of friends); note industry, closeness, client/intro/insight potential (Nate Herk 061).
2. **Days 2–3:** 5–10 warm, low-pressure, non-pitch discovery conversations: "I'm not trying to sell you anything. Could I just ask you a few questions about where things feel manual or annoying in your day-to-day?"
3. **Days 4–5:** Turn one conversation into a tiny FREE pilot — one small automation on the clearest painful task, one measured outcome, in exchange for honest feedback.
4. **Days 5–6:** Build a tiny MVP (simpler the better — goal is showing time saved, not impressive tech). Capture client's own language about problem and win.
5. **Day 7:** No hard close — propose maintenance (keep it working, fix breaks, tweaks) or expansion (add 1–2 features). Ask for testimonial/referral only AFTER delivered proof.
6. Repeat cycle multiple times before cold outreach. Defer cold outreach and Upwork until after several proof cycles (Nate Herk 061).

**Cold outreach (deferred until proof):** requires high volume, specific offer + niche, 4+ months runway, high quit rate (Albert Olgaard 028 took 4 months; Carson Reed used paid Meta ads to amplify — ~$677 ad spend for ~$4,500 collected, self-reported). Not a week-1 tactic.

# PART 8 — SALES

**Exactly how we sell: discovery-first, diagnose-then-prescribe.**

1. Call one: map the client's pain in time/money/focus (Nate Herk 062).
2. Before call two: build a short audit/wireframe/process map.
3. Call two: present "here's what we mapped → here's the solution → am I understanding you correctly?"
4. Doctor analogy: prospects don't buy workflows (hundreds are free) — they buy diagnosis + prescription. No hard sell once pain is crystal clear; make the cost of pain bigger than cost of solution.
5. Fully scope the project; define completion/deliverable in contract to avoid disputes on final payment (Liam Ottley 017).
6. Communication: clear, show proof (past builds, personal builds, testimonials) to establish reasonable likelihood of delivery.

**Objections handled:** "AI doesn't matter to owners — only time, money, focus." Market distrust from vendors who overcharge/overpromise/underdeliver or go too cheap. Big consulting firms charge hundreds of thousands for audits — SMBs can't afford them, move faster, decide quicker — that's the opening (Nate Herk 062).

# PART 9 — DELIVERY

**Exactly how we fulfill: planning → building → reviewing → deploying → handing over (Liam Ottley 007).**

1. **Planning:** identify needs/chatbot value; gather requirements (Q&A knowledge base, conversation flow, capture fields, conversion events).
2. **Building:** Botpress nodes/transitions/messages/variables + custom code blocks; Stack AI via API for KB queries; fallback loop so bot never dead-ends; re-push conversion after every answer.
3. **Reviewing:** send testing URL; client stress-tests; batch feedback into ONE iteration round (not constant back-and-forth — becomes a nightmare with many clients).
4. **Deploying:** client creates Botpress account, adds builder as collaborator, builder transfers bot to client's account; client billed/responsible.
5. **Handing over:** bot lives on client's account; embed via web-chat script URL on client's website.

**Mindset shifts (Nick Puru 036):**
- Point solutions → infrastructure (build unified "AI core" incrementally via quick wins)
- Named problem is usually a symptom; real problem is upstream
- Price by what the problem costs, not what AI can do (ROI model before building)
- AI transformation is a people problem, not technical (involve team from day one; frame AI as augmentation)

# PART 13 — FIRST $1K

**Exact progression:**

# PART 14 — $3K/MONTH

**What changes:**

- 1–2 paid retainer clients at $300–$1,000/mo maintenance (Liam Ottley 007).
- Move from one-off projects to recurring maintenance ("maintenance is mandatory, not optional, and is the recurring-revenue engine" — Andrew Dunn 024).
- Add upsells: additional automations, audits, workshops (chatbot as Trojan horse — Liam Ottley 007).
- Begin Cost Plus pricing with 2x markup; scale to 3x as experience grows.
- Build SOPs for the quick-win delivery (Nick Puru 036).

**Success condition:** ~$3,000/mo recurring (mix of project + maintenance).

# PART 15 — $5K/MONTH

**What changes:**

- 2–4 clients, mix of projects ($2,000–$3,000 each) + retainers ($300–$1,000/mo).
- Add audit/assessment as entry offer ($5K–15K — Eliot Prince 025).
- Delegate simple builds to a developer ($30–50/hr) using Cost Plus markup (Liam Ottley 017; Eliot Prince 025 partnering/white-labeling).
- Document SOPs; free frameworks (discovery audit template, ROI calculator, prioritization matrix) as lead magnets (Nick Puru 036).

**Success condition:** ~$5,000/mo recurring with delegated delivery starting.

# PART 16 — $10K/MONTH

**What changes:**

- 3–6 clients; retainers dominate revenue.
- Hire 2–3 developers and SOPs once near ~$10K/month (Liam Ottley 020).
- Five-layer AI core (data foundation → intelligence engine → orchestration → integration framework → command center) — Nick Puru 036.
- Quick-win infrastructure: $10K/$8K/few-thousand per quick win; $50K → seven automations in complete infrastructure vs $10K per isolated automation (Nick Puru 036).
- Retainer target $3,000–$8,000/mo per client once you have 10–20 automations/deliverables (Liam Ottley 007 — unverified forward claim, directional only).

**Success condition:** ~$10,000/mo recurring with delegated delivery.

# PART 17 — SYSTEMIZATION

**How delivery becomes repeatable:**

- SOPs for each quick-win delivery (discovery, build, review, deploy, handover).
- Free frameworks as lead magnets: discovery audit template, ROI calculator, prioritization matrix, interview questions (Nick Puru 036).
- Batch client feedback into one iteration round (Liam Ottley 007).
- ROI model built before anything is built; first-year benefits conservatively at ~60% of potential so you "exceed projections, not fall short" (Nick Puru 036).
- Price by what the problem costs, not what AI can do (Nick Puru 036).

# PART 20 — WHAT NOT TO DO

**Major traps (from contradictions.md and synthesis):**

- Do NOT start with SaaS/software from $0 (unanimous: service first).
- Do NOT pick a niche "because there's money in it" — pick the one you can reach (4-factor audit, Liam Ottley 015).
- Do NOT cold outreach as week-1 tactic (defer until after 1–2 paid proof clients; high quit rate, requires volume + 4+ months — Albert Olgaard 028).
- Do NOT treat $10k/month in 90 days or $1M AIAA in 30 days as typical (marketing framing; Zubair Trabzada 009 puts guru-income-claim odds at ~0.1%).
- Do NOT bite off more than you can chew on the first client — stick to chatbots, bounded scope (Liam Ottley 007).
- Do NOT rely solely on YouTube/personal brand for leads (Liam Ottley was building outbound because of brand dependence — 007).
- Do NOT treat unverified income claims as proven — only Liam Ottley's $1,000 Stripe proof is documented (007).
- Do NOT skip discovery/diagnosis — "you can't automate what you don't understand" (Nick Puru 036).
- Do NOT offer constant back-and-forth with clients — batch feedback into one iteration round (Liam Ottley 007).

# PART 22 — DAILY/WEEKLY OPERATING SYSTEM

**CURRENT STAGE:** Stage 4 — Getting the first customer.

**OBJECTIVE:** Land the FIRST paid AI-automation client (case study + testimonial + proof of delivery) within 30–60 days. Target: 1 paid client at $1,000–$2,000, then a second, then move to paid retainers.

**DO THIS NOW:**
1. Build trust map: Google Sheet of 20 people where trust already exists (friends/family running businesses, past co-workers, managers, clients, community contacts).
2. Run 5–10 warm discovery conversations (non-pitch): "where things feel manual or annoying in your day-to-day?"
3. Turn one conversation into a tiny FREE pilot (one measurable outcome: hours saved, errors reduced, leads captured).
4. Build tiny MVP showing time saved.
5. Propose maintenance or expansion (no hard close); get testimonial after delivered proof.

**THEN DO THIS:**
6. First PAID client at $1,000 case-study rate (discounted from $2,000–$3,000).
7. Collect Stripe proof + testimonial + documented result.
8. Second client at $2,000–$3,000 typical using Cost Plus pricing.
9. Move to maintenance retainers ($300–$1,000/mo).

**DO NOT BUILD YET:** SaaS, custom code, employees, paid ads, paid outreach tooling, complex infrastructure. All deferred until after proof + revenue.

**DO NOT CHANGE DIRECTION UNLESS:** zero warm contacts after outreach (build trust map first), unable to receive payment from Kenya (change payment method/local currency), or no paid client after 20+ trust-map conversations + 2 free pilots (re-pick niche via 4-factor audit).

**SUCCESS CONDITION:** 1 paid client at $1,000–$2,000 + 1 testimonial + documented result + Stripe/ payment proof.

**NEXT STAGE:** Stage 5 — Delivering the result (planning→build→review→deploy→handover with Botpress + Stack AI), then Stage 6 — Getting proof.
