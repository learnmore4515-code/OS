---

## 1. Executive Summary / Thesis

**One-sentence thesis:** Position Kim/Peter's Fiverr gig as an outcome-first, insurance-niche-specific offer — **"I will set up a working AI appointment-booking and lead-capture chatbot for your insurance brokerage in 7 days"** — leading with a niche-specific outcome rather than a generic tool (n8n/GPT) mention, and back it with a short demo video so the gig converts on proof instead of price.

**Core principle:** Buyers buy outcomes, not technology. Commodity titles like "I will build n8n AI automation" lose to outcome-first, niche-specific headlines like "I will build a WhatsApp AI chatbot for medical clinics healthcare appointment booking."

## 2. Agency Context (from Agent 1)

**Business model:** Done-for-you AI Automation Agency (AIAA) — service-first, not software. Productized done-for-you offer: outward-facing AI chatbot (lead nurturing, lead capture, conversion-event push, customer support via custom knowledge base), AI audit/assessment, or done-for-you automation.

**Niche:** Insurance brokers in Kenya (chosen via 4-factor unfair-advantage audit — business access / existing relationships).

**Tech stack:** Botpress (chatbot logic, flow, deployment, web-chat embed) + Stack AI via API (knowledge base document search) + n8n / Make.com (workflow automation). Figma for flow diagramming. Client takes ownership of their Botpress account; builder transfers the bot to it.

**Current stage:** Stage 4 — first customer / problem discovery. Zero paid clients yet. Objective: first paid client at $1,000–$2,000 within 30–60 days ($1,000 discounted case-study rate vs. $2,000–$3,000 typical).

**Verified broker pains:** renewal follow-ups/notices (2mo/1mo/1wk/3d sequence), chasing signed documents and submission info, manual email follow-ups, premium reconciliation (M-Pesa unallocated payments), comparative quotation creation (~2–3 hours per quote manually), policy admin (~60% of broker time), onboarding integration gaps.

**Constraints (must not be relaxed):** No cold outreach until 1–2 paid proof clients. No SaaS/custom code/employees/paid ads until proof + revenue. Must verify Stripe/USD receipt from Kenya or price in local currency.

**Fiverr reality check (from Agent 1):** Peter's playbook pricing ($1,000–$3,000/project) sits far above Fiverr's commoditized Automations & Workflows band ($120–$140 average per Fiverr's own FAQ; gigs seen from $10–$250). Fiverr is not in the current execution plan — it's deferred until after proof. If used later, it should be for **lead flow only**, not as a pricing anchor.

**Top 3 positioning patterns:**

1. **Outcome-first, niche-specific headlines win.** "I will set up a working AI booking agent for your salon in 14 days" beats "I will build n8n AI automation" because buyers buy outcomes, not technology. Name the niche (insurance brokers) and the outcome (booking/lead capture) explicitly.

2. **Review volume + seller badge is the primary trust signal.** Every verified top seller leads with review counts (450+, 350+, 310+, 285, 177, 145). With zero reviews, Peter must substitute proof (demo video, before/after, specific metric) and price aggressively to buy the first 10–15 reviews quickly, then raise prices every ~10 reviews.

3. **Three-tier ladder anchored on outcome, middle tier as "most popular."** Basic entry → Standard target sale → Premium price ceiling; the "most popular" Standard tier converts 60–70% of buyers. Price by outcome, not hours.

**Strategy:** Start at the bottom of these ranges to accumulate 10–15 reviews within the first month, then raise prices every ~10 reviews. Use gig extras ($20–$50) for WhatsApp/CRM integration or faster delivery.

**Gig extras ($20–$50 each):** WhatsApp Business API integration, CRM integration (HubSpot/GHL), expedited delivery (+2 days), custom walkthrough video, post-setup training session

### 5.5 Gig Description Template

**Hook:**
I help insurance brokers stop losing leads to slow follow-up. I'll set up a working AI chatbot that books appointments and captures leads from WhatsApp and your website — trained on your brokerage info, live in 7 days.

**Process (tools mentioned briefly):**
Built with n8n, Make.com, WhatsApp Business API, and Stack AI knowledge base. You get a live, working automation — not a tutorial.

**Deliverables:**
- AI appointment-booking chatbot trained on your brokerage content
- Lead capture form + qualification (name, email, phone, insurance type)
- WhatsApp Business API integration for client messaging
- One-page walkthrough video of the automation running
- Client handoff and training session

**Why me:**
I specialize in insurance brokerage automation. My bots are trained on actual broker workflows — renewal follow-ups, document chasing, quotation comparison — not generic scripts. I deliver working automations, then hand over to your account so you own it.

**FAQ:**
- *Do I need technical skills?* No. I handle build, testing, and handover. You receive a working chatbot link.
- *Which platforms does it work on?* WhatsApp, your website, and email. Integrates with your existing CRM or Google Sheets.
- *Can it speak other languages?* Yes — the bot auto-detects and replies in the customer's language.
- *What happens after 7 days?* You own the automation on your account. I transfer access and provide a training session.
- *Is this just a demo?* No — it's a working chatbot connected to your WhatsApp Business API and lead capture destination.

### 5.6 Requirements (what buyers need to provide)

1. Your WhatsApp Business API number (or permission to set one up)
2. Your website URL or business documents (FAQs, services, pricing info)
3. Where you want leads sent (email address or Google Sheets)
4. Your brand colors (optional, for widget styling)
5. Preferred appointment scheduling tool (Calendly or similar)

### 5.7 Gallery / Media Requirements

## 6. Launch Strategy

**Phase 1 — Credibility building (first 3–5 orders):**
- Price at bottom of recommended range: Basic $30 / Standard $150 / Premium $400
- Deliver early, over-communicate scope, respond to inquiries within 24 hours
- Ask for review right after buyer confirms satisfaction
- Target: 10–15 reviews within first month

**Phase 2 — Price escalation (after 10–15 reviews):**
- Raise prices every ~10 reviews
- Move toward anchor prices: Basic $49 / Standard $149 / Premium $399
- Add gig extras for WhatsApp/CRM integration ($20–$50 each)

**Phase 3 — Integration with playbook (after 3–5 Fiverr reviews):**
- Use Fiverr gig as social proof / credibility layer in warm insurance-broker outreach
- Primary paid client ($1,000–$2,000) still comes from warm LinkedIn discovery → free pilot → paid client path per the playbook
- Fiverr serves as lead flow supplement, not primary channel

## 7. What NOT to Do (Commodity Traps)

- Don't charge $5–$50 for generic AI output — you'll fight for crumbs
- Don't promise "access to ChatGPT" — promise a working deliverable
- Don't target broad "small businesses" — target insurance brokers specifically
- Don't deliver raw AI output — add human polish, source files, before/after samples
- Don't use tool-only titles like "n8n expert" or "automation expert"
- Don't price by hours — price by outcome
