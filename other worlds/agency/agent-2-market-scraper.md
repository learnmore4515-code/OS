## 2. Gig title patterns that convert

Winning titles follow "I will [verb] [outcome] with [tool stack]" — they lead with the business result and stack tool names after, never just "X expert":

- "I will build ai agent with rag langchain langgraph and ai automation with n8n make" (From $200, Fiverr's Choice, 5.0/25)
- "I will setup n8n make com ai automation with API vapi retell ai voice calling agent" (From $35, Level 2, 5.0/55)
- "I will be your n8n, make, zapier ai automation expert" (From $20, Level 2, 5.0/14)
- "I will build whatsapp ai chatbot for medical clinics healthcare appointment booking" (From $200) — niche-specific
- "I will build a custom rag chatbot for documents and knowledgebase" (From $70)

Patterns to avoid: vague tool-only titles like "n8n expert" or "automation expert"; generic "I will do ai automation" without a stated outcome.

## 5. Gallery/image/video best practices for expert positioning

- Gallery: real worksample screenshots of the actual n8n/Make canvas, not generic illustrations or logos (observed on live gig pages; portfolio links per seller, e.g. fiverr.com/users/<username>/portfolio).
- Profile picture: a real photo of your face converts better than a logo/illustration.
- Video: record a 60–90 second screen-capture of the automation running; deliver a ~2-minute walkthrough video with every project.
- Show before/after: manual process screenshot → automated workflow running.
- Many top sellers offer "video consultations" as a gig extra — signals expertise and raises order value.

## 6. How new sellers get their first orders on Fiverr

- Price the first 2–3 orders for volume, not profit (lower end: Basic $25–$50), then move toward middle/upper after 5–10 reviews.
- Respond quickly to inquiries and buyer briefs; Fiverr tracks response rate.
- Over-communicate: confirm scope explicitly in the brief before starting.
- Ask for a review right after the buyer confirms satisfaction.
- Deliver slightly ahead of the stated timeline when possible.
- Build 2–3 portfolio pieces before the first paying client.
- New sellers without reviews should undercut established sellers slightly while matching their scope, then raise prices once rated 4.9–5.0.
