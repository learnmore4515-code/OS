# Leo Pisha — Three High-Leverage Growth Experiments

Selected from the Opportunity Register and Competitive Map. Each experiment targets a real, evidence-backed gap and maps to a required category:
- Experiment 1 = free practical tool (required category)
- Experiment 2 = distinctive demonstration / original research (required category)
- Experiment 3 = distribution / industry authority (required category)

All three are zero or minimal cost. None touches Session 1's production website unless the user approves integration.

---

## Experiment 1 — Free claims follow-up / missing-document tracker (tool)

**Target customer:** Kenyan insurance brokers and claims operations teams who chase clients for missing claim documents.

**Exact problem:** Claims stall waiting on documents (police abstracts, medical reports, proof of loss). Ops teams chase clients manually via WhatsApp/phone with no visibility into which claims are stuck or what's missing. This is the high-frequency manual pain behind "claims from weeks to hours."

**Why it's better than competitors:** No competitor offers a free interactive tool (smartbiz sells WhatsApp bots; rensoft/vaatun sell platforms; aiconsultant/digital4africa/qenai sell consulting). A free tool is zero-risk for the broker and demonstrates Leo Pisha's insurance expertise directly.

**Proposed solution:** A single-page, client-side web tool: log a claim, select required documents, mark which are missing, get suggested follow-up reminders and a printable summary. Built as static HTML/JS (no backend), hosted free.

**Minimum viable implementation:**
- Single HTML page with claim entry form, document checklist, missing-doc summary, and "book a discovery call" CTA.
- Client-side only (localStorage), no user accounts, no backend.
- Mobile-friendly (brokers work on phones).
- Clearly label: illustrative tool, not a system of record.

**Required tools & estimated cost:** None (text editor, free static hosting e.g. GitHub Pages or Vercel hobby). Estimated cost: KES 0.

**Execution time:** 1–2 weeks (build + test + document limitations).

**How customers discover it:** LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp shares to broker contacts/groups (existing relationships only), SEO on "claims follow up tracker Kenya" / "insurance missing documents tracker" (hand to Session 1 for on-site page after approval).

**How it supports organic search:** A dedicated tool page targets long-tail, high-intent insurance-ops queries competitors don't address.

**How it produces qualified enquiries/revenue:** CTA to book a discovery call; email capture for the research report (Experiment 2); tool users are in-market buyers with an active claims pain.

**Success metrics:**
- Tool sessions / unique users in first 30 days
- "Book a discovery call" clicks from the tool
- Email captures
- Consultation bookings attributed to the tool

**Main risks & assumptions:**
- Brokers may not adopt a new tool without a trusted referral (mitigate via WhatsApp to existing contacts).
- Assumes document chasing is a real, felt pain (supported by competitor service lists; verify with 2–3 broker interviews before/after launch).

## Experiment 2 — "Cost of manual claims work in Kenyan insurance" report (original research / demonstration)

**Target customer:** Insurance CEOs, COOs, operations heads, and brokers who approve automation budgets.

**Exact problem:** No published Kenyan-specific data on how much manual claims/underwriting work costs. Buyers make automation decisions without benchmarks, which slows purchases.

**Why it's better than competitors:** No competitor publishes original Kenyan insurance operations research (rensoft has software testimonials; smartbiz quotes unattributed % claims). A transparent, honest report positions Leo Pisha as the market authority — and mirrors aiconsultant's credible "we do not publish invented statistics" stance.

**Proposed solution:** A 3–5 page report based on 5–8 structured interviews with insurance ops teams: hours spent per claim, cost per manual touchpoint, renewal lapse estimates, biggest bottlenecks. Clearly state sample size, methodology, and that figures are illustrative interview findings — not industry statistics.

**Minimum viable implementation:**
- 5 structured interviews (30 mins each) with broker/insurer ops contacts.
- 3–5 page PDF report with methodology, limitations, and findings.
- Honest limitations section (small sample, not representative).

**Required tools & estimated cost:** None (call/notebook, PDF export). Estimated cost: KES 0.

**Execution time:** 3–6 weeks (interviews, write, review).

**How customers discover it:** LinkedIn distribution, TechTrendsKE or similar Kenyan business publication, PIBA/Insurance Institute of Kenya channels (Experiment 3), gated download on leopisha.co.ke (hand to Session 1 after approval).

**How it supports organic search:** Report page targets "cost of manual claims processing Kenya," "insurance operations efficiency Kenya," etc.

**How it produces qualified enquiries/revenue:** Gated download captures emails of budget-holders; report concludes with invitation to book a free workflow diagnosis.

**Success metrics:**
- Report downloads
- Email captures from download
- Consultation bookings attributed to the report
- Any media or association pickup

**Main risks & assumptions:**
- Hard to get ops leaders to interview (mitigate via existing Leo Pisha network and warm intros).
- Risk of overstating findings (mitigate with strict methodology + limitations labels).
- Assumes buyers care about cost benchmarks (validate in interviews).

## Experiment 3 — Contributed article / webinar via PIBA or Insurance Institute of Kenya (authority / distribution)

**Target customer:** Insurance brokers and professionals (PIBA / IIK members).

**Exact problem:** Insurance professionals know automation exists but don't know it's accessible, affordable, or compliant with Kenyan regulatory context (IRA licence calendar, data protection). Leo Pisha has no third-party authority signal yet.

**Why it's better than competitors:** Competitors sell products/platforms; none positions as a Kenya-regulatory-aware automation authority. A contributed piece to the broker association builds trust faster than self-published content.

**Proposed solution:** One contributed article (e.g. "Automating claims without replacing your team — a practical guide for Kenyan brokers") or one 30-minute webinar for PIBA/IIK members. Compliance-aware, practical, no vendor hard-sell.

**Minimum viable implementation:**
- One article submitted to PIBA or Insurance Institute of Kenya newsletter/blog, OR one webinar delivered to their members.
- Author bio with link to leopisha.co.ke and a book-a-call CTA.

**Required tools & estimated cost:** None. Estimated cost: KES 0 (time to write/prepare).

**Execution time:** 2–4 weeks (pitch, write, publish/present).

**How customers discover it:** PIBA/IIK newsletter, member emails, event attendance.

**How it supports organic search:** Earns a backlink from a relevant industry site to leopisha.co.ke (Session 1 benefits).

**How it produces qualified enquiries/revenue:** Article/webinar readers who have a claims pain book consultations; association credibility shortens the sales cycle.

**Success metrics:**
- Article published / webinar delivered
- Webinar attendees (if applicable)
- Referral enquiries citing PIBA/IIK
- Backlink to leopisha.co.ke

**Main risks & assumptions:**
- Association may not accept contributed content (mitigate: pitch to both PIBA and Insurance Institute of Kenya; have a fallback of publishing on LinkedIn and leopisha.co.ke).
- Assumes brokers attend/read association content (reasonable, given vaatun's compliance focus on IRA licences).

## Ranking & sequencing

1. **Experiment 1 (free tool)** first — fastest to ship, zero cost, direct lead capture.
2. **Experiment 4-adjacent productized service (diagnosis + roadmap)** in parallel with Experiment 1 — gives tool visitors something to buy.
3. **Experiment 2 (research report)** after 5+ interviews — don't publish without real data.
4. **Experiment 3 (authority)** once the tool and report exist (have something credible to contribute).

**Zero/minimal-cost items:** All four (tool, diagnosis service, report, article) are KES 0 out-of-pocket; cost is time only.
