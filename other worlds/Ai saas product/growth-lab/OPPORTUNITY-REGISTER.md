# Leo Pisha — Opportunity Register

Growth assets beyond blog posts. Each entry states what it is, who it serves, the evidence it rests on, and how it could produce enquiries or revenue. Companion to Session 1's production website work — recommendations only, no website changes.

Source: COMPETITIVE-MAP.md (competitor profiles and gaps G1–G6).

## A. Free tools

| Tool | What it does | Who it serves | Why it's a strong initial opportunity | Cost | How it produces enquiries |
|------|-------------|---------------|--------------------------------------|------|---------------------------|
| Claims follow-up / missing-document tracker | Log a claim, list required documents, mark what's missing, get suggested follow-up reminders | Kenyan insurance brokers & claims ops teams | Gap G3 (no competitor has a free tool) + G5 (document chasing stalls claims). Directly demonstrates Leo Pisha's core value (claims from weeks to hours). | Near zero (static client-side page) | Embed "book a discovery call" CTA; capture email for report; tool users are in-market buyers |
| Renewal reminder calendar | Simple calendar/ checklist for policy renewal dates, with IRA licence calendar note (renewal due 30 Sept, expires 31 Dec — per vaatun) | Insurance brokers & agencies | Addresses renewal lapse (smartbiz cites 20–30% annual revenue loss from lapse). High-frequency, low-trust-barrier touchpoint. | Near zero | Capture broker email; upsell renewal automation / managed ops |
| KYC checklist / document completeness checker | Checklist of documents required for a client onboarding / claim, with completeness score | Insurance brokers, agents, bancassurance | Document collection is a recurring manual burden across onboarding and claims. | Near zero | Same lead-capture pattern; positions Leo Pisha as operations-aware |
| Premium reconciliation template | Spreadsheet/template structure for reconciling premiums collected (M-Pesa STK Push) against policies issued | Finance teams at brokers/underwriters | aiconsultant cites M-Pesa reconciliation (86% clean automation) as a pain point. Tangible, downloadable, useful immediately. | Near zero | Download gate = email + consultation interest |
| General business follow-up tracker | Generic follow-up/reminder tool (not insurance-specific) | Any Kenyan small business | Weakest fit: broad, undifferentiated, competes with many generic tools. Not recommended as the lead tool. | Near zero | Low intent; poor lead quality |

**Recommended first tool:** claims follow-up / missing-document tracker. It is the most insurance-specific, most painful, and most directly tied to Leo Pisha's core service (claims automation). Build one tool well rather than five mediocre ones.

## C. Original research

| Asset | What it is | What the evidence supports | What needs more research | How to avoid misleading generalization |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| "The cost of manual claims work in Kenyan insurance" report | 3–5 page report based on 5–8 structured interviews with insurance ops teams; hours per claim, cost per manual touchpoint, renewal lapse estimates | Confirmed that Kenyan insurers/brokers process claims manually (competitor service lists, smartbiz FNOL + renewal reminder features, vaatun broker workflow focus). KES 1.1 trillion sector size (NTT DATA) shows market scale. | Actual hours-per-claim and cost-per-touchpoint figures for Kenyan firms; renewal lapse rates beyond smartbiz's unattributed 20–30%. | Label all figures as illustrative interview findings, not industry statistics; state sample size and methodology; say "we do not publish invented statistics" (mirrors aiconsultant's honest approach, which is a credibility differentiator). |

**Verdict:** original research is viable but requires real interviews before publishing. Do not fabricate statistics. Start with 5 interviews; if they don't surface consistent themes, narrow the scope or drop it.

Build **one free tool (claims follow-up / missing-document tracker)** + **one productized service (diagnosis + roadmap)** in parallel, then run the **original research report** once 5+ interviews are done. Hand the tool and research to Session 1 for on-site integration only after the user approves.
