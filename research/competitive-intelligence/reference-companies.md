# Reference Companies — Competitive Intelligence (Phase 2)

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Method:** Homepages inspected via content fetch (WebFetch). Observed features are reported verbatim where quoted; nothing is fabricated.  
**Limitation:** No general web-browser screenshot tooling was available (only local dev-server preview tooling, which requires `.claude/launch.json` that does not exist), so screenshots of reference sites could not be captured. Feature observations below come from fetched page content, not screenshots. Deep pages (pricing, docs, blog, community) were NOT fully crawled — this is a homepage-level inventory only.

## Coverage note

This set covers every category named in the brief: workflow automation agencies/platforms (n8n, Zapier, Make), AI agent platforms (LangChain), developer-first technology companies (Vercel), and enterprise consultancies (Thoughtworks). It does NOT include enterprise AI consultancies such as McKinsey QuantumBlack, BCG X, or Accenture, whose sites were not fetched. Findings below should be treated as directional, drawn from homepages only.

## Source handling

Exact quotes and metrics are attributed to the fetched homepage content. No traffic numbers, conversion rates, or customer counts beyond what appeared on the page are claimed. Where a homepage did not reveal a detail (e.g. specific pricing tiers), that is reported as "not visible on homepage" rather than inferred.
