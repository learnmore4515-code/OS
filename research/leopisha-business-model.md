# LeoPisha Business Model — Phase 5

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Basis:** Phase 3 strategic positioning (hybrid: specialist insurance consultancy + free resources + productized implementation), Phase 1 audit, Phase 2 competitive intelligence.  
**Core rule:** Consultancy revenue stays central. Free resources create demand for paid expertise; they do not replace it. No subscriptions merely to imitate software companies.

---

## Revenue model overview (four layers)

### Layer 1 — Free (demand generation, not revenue)
Educational resources, lightweight tools, templates, demonstrations, and public knowledge that any visitor can use without talking to LeoPisha.

| Free asset | What it is | Purpose | Paid demand it creates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource guides (3–5 at launch) | Problem-organized guides (e.g., "which workflows to automate," "when automation helps vs. staying manual") | Capture informational-search visitors; demonstrate expertise | Automation Diagnosis for readers who realize their process needs help |
| Free automation-readiness assessment | Lightweight questionnaire/scoring tool (Tier 2) | Low-friction first value; captures visitors not ready to book | Diagnosis for those scoring "high manual drag" |
| Public n8n templates | Sanitized workflow templates (claims intake, renewal reminders, document follow-up) (Tier 2) | Show competence; attract n8n practitioners and referral traffic | Implementation/hand-over for buyers who want it customized |
| Honest stage statement + demo data (clearly labeled) | Transparency about where the firm is | Builds trust without fabrication | N/A (trust, not direct revenue) |

**Which resources stay free:** Everything above. Nothing here requires a sales call. The free tier's job is to be useful on its own *and* to make the paid option obvious when the visitor outgrows it.

### Layer 2 — Productized services (clearly scoped, fixed deliverables)
Fixed-scope implementations with defined deliverables, boundaries, and outcomes. Sold at fixed prices (or fixed ranges), not hourly. These are the bread-and-butter.

| Productized service | Scope | Deliverables | Timeline | Pricing basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Automation Diagnosis | Map workflows, quantify manual-work cost, prioritize roadmap, identify quick wins | Process audit report, ROI model, automation roadmap, quick-win identification | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee |
| Claims processing automation | FNOL intake → document ingestion → AI extraction → triage → auto-approval → adjuster dashboard | n8n workflows, AI document processing, triage engine, adjuster dashboard, compliance logging | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee (fixed scope) |
| Underwriting automation | Quote-to-bind for motor/property/SME; risk scoring; straight-through processing; exception routing | Risk scoring models, broker portal, STP workflows, exception handling, reinsurance reporting | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee |
| Customer operations automation | 24/7 support via WhatsApp/portal; eligibility, claims status, benefits, provider search | WhatsApp bot, RAG knowledge base, self-service portal, smart escalation, analytics dashboard | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee |
| Missing document & KYC reminder automation | Automated reminders, document tracking, escalation routing | Document reminder workflows, KYC tracking, escalation routing, client templates | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee |
| Insurance renewal reminder automation | Renewal reminders, policy-expiry tracking, renewal follow-up | Renewal workflows, expiry tracking, follow-up scheduling, retention reporting | 1–2 weeks | Fixed fee |

**Boundaries:** Each productized service has a defined in-scope and out-of-scope. Out-of-scope work (custom integrations, multi-system architecture, regulatory builds beyond Kenya DPA basics) is quoted as consultancy, not folded into the fixed price.

### Layer 3 — Consultancy (higher-value, custom)
Custom architecture, complex integrations, multi-system operational improvements, and work that exceeds productized scope.

| Consultancy engagement | What it covers | Pricing basis |
|---|---|---|
| Custom automation architecture | Multi-system integration, custom n8n nodes, API builds beyond standard connectors | Scoped project fee |
| Complex operational improvement | Cross-department workflow redesign, change management, regulatory-context builds | Scoped project fee |
| Implementation beyond standard scope | Work that exceeds a productized service's boundaries | Hourly or scoped project fee (quoted after diagnosis) |

### Layer 4 — Recurring revenue (genuine continuing need only)
Maintenance, monitoring, optimization, support — sold only where the customer has an actual ongoing need, not invented for subscription mimicry.

| Recurring service | When it applies | Pricing basis |
|---|---|---|
| Managed Automation Operations | Customer owns the system but wants LeoPisha to run/optimize it; monthly reviews, proactive improvements, priority support, SLA monitoring | Monthly retainer |
| Monitoring & maintenance | Workflows in production that need uptime monitoring, failure alerting, periodic review | Monthly retainer |
| Optimization | Quarterly workflow review and refinement as the client's process changes | Quarterly or per-engagement |

**Rule:** Recurring revenue is offered only when there is a real continuing need (production workflows that break, need monitoring, or need process updates). It is never sold as a subscription for access to tools or content.

### Layer 5 — Optional digital products (only with credible market evidence)
Paid templates, implementation kits, or other reusable assets. Not built unless research demonstrates a market.

| Digital product | Condition to build |
|---|---|
| Paid implementation kits | Only after free templates prove demand and customers ask for a paid, supported version |
| Industry-specific automation playbooks | Only after a body of real case studies exists |

**Status:** Not built at launch. Deferred until there is evidence of demand.

## How free offerings create demand for paid consultancy (anti-cannibalization)

| Free asset | Why it doesn't cannibalize paid work | Where it hands off to paid |
|---|---|---|
| Resource guides | Teach principles, not the client's specific process. A guide tells you *what* to automate; diagnosis tells you *your* exact workflow and cost. | Reader books diagnosis to apply it to their business |
| Free assessment | Self-scored, generic. Produces a score and a recommendation, not a process audit report or ROI model. | High-scoring visitors book diagnosis for a real roadmap |
| Public n8n templates | Generic, sanitized, self-serve. Not customized to the client's systems, data, or compliance context. | Buyers book implementation to adapt, integrate, and hand over |
| Honest stage statement | Builds trust, delivers no implementation | N/A |

**Cannibalization guardrails:**
- Free tools are self-serve and generic; they never produce a client-specific roadmap, ROI model, or production workflow.
- The diagnosis is the gate to paid work: it's the point where LeoPisha maps *the client's* actual process. Free content leads *to* diagnosis, not away from it.
- Productized services have hard scope boundaries; anything outside is consultancy (quoted separately).
- No free tier of a paid product exists — there is no paid product to cannibalize at launch.

---

## Pricing philosophy

- **No SaaS-style pricing tiers.** There is no "Starter / Pro / Enterprise" for software access because LeoPisha is not selling software access.
- **Fixed fees for productized services** (predictable for the buyer, scoped for the firm).
- **Quoted fees for diagnosis, consultancy, and recurring** (scoping-dependent).
- Prices are not published as tiers; diagnosis pricing is discussed in the consultation (consistent with the existing "you decide what to build" model).
- No auto-renewing subscriptions for content or tools.

---

## What is explicitly NOT in the model

- Subscription SaaS product (no recurring access fees).
- Marketplace transaction fees (no expert directory taking commissions at launch).
- Affiliate or referral revenue as a core pillar (may appear later, never core).
- Advertising or sponsored content.
- Paid certification or training programs (unless real demand is proven).

---

## Revenue mix at launch (illustrative, not a forecast)

- **Layer 2 productized services:** primary revenue (Diagnosis → fixed-scope implementation).
- **Layer 3 consultancy:** secondary, for out-of-scope and custom work.
- **Layer 4 recurring:** minimal at launch; grows only as production workflows need monitoring.
- **Layers 1 and 5:** $0 direct revenue at launch (demand generation and deferred, respectively).
